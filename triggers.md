## Commands

|Command|Example|Permission|Description|
|-|-|-|-|
|`!addquote`|!addquote Hello, World!|VIP, Mod, Broadcaster|Creates a new quote with the given text and responds with the quote's ID|
|`!deletequote`|!deletequote 1|Mod, Broadcaster|Deletes the quote and responds with a confirmation|
|`!discord`, `!dc`|!dc|Chatter|Responds with a link to Stone's Discord server|
|`!editquote`|!editquote 1 Hello, World!|Mod, Broadcaster|Changes the quote to have the given text and responds with a confirmation|
|`!feedrecord`|!feedrecord|Chatter|Responds with the current feed record count and record holder|
|`!feed`|!feed|Chatter|Feeds the cat|
|`!lurk`|!lurk|Chatter|Responds with a thank you|
|`!quote`|!quote 1|Chatter|Responds with the quote|
|`!youtube`, `!yt`|!yt|Chatter|Responds with a link to Stone's YouTube channel|
